Contact & support

BiDecks Support

Need help saving a source, finding something in your Vault, or using Full Access? Find quick answers below or contact the Team5 team behind BiDecks. No account or sign-in is needed to get help.

Contact support

For app issues, bugs, purchase problems, or questions about how BiDecks works, email support@bidecks.com. If the link does not open an email app, copy the address into your preferred email service.

To help us investigate, please include:

  • What happened and what you expected to happen.
  • The steps that lead to the problem and any error message.
  • Your device model and iOS, iPadOS, or Android version.
  • Your BiDecks version, shown in Settings > About.
  • A screenshot or an example source link, if useful and safe to share. Remove private information first.

Do not send passwords, payment-card details, or your complete Vault backup. We do not need access to your Apple or Google account to help you.

Where to find help in the app

Open Settings > Getting started for an introduction, or Settings > How to / FAQ for detailed instructions on saving, Decks, Topics, Try Lists, search, and app settings. Settings > Contact & support opens the same support email.

BiDecks is missing from the share sheet

In the source app, open Share, then look in More or the apps list for BiDecks. Your device may let you add it to favourites. Alternatively, copy the source link, open BiDecks > Vault > Add link, and press Paste.

A link has no title or thumbnail

Check your internet connection and whether the original link opens in its source app or browser. Some platforms restrict previews, and private, removed, or unavailable sources may not provide metadata. If source details are unavailable, you can still add your own title or note and save the link.

If the problem continues, email support with the source platform, the error message, and a public example link if possible.

Back up or move your Vault

Your Vault is stored locally on your device, not in a BiDecks cloud account. Open Settings > Backup & transfer > Export vault to create a file containing your Vault and attachments, then keep it somewhere secure.

Import vault replaces the Vault currently on the destination device. Export that device's Vault first if you need to keep it, and read the import confirmation carefully. Backup files are not encrypted.

Before uninstalling BiDecks or resetting your device, export a backup. Removing the app can delete its local data. Support cannot retrieve a Vault from a BiDecks server; recovery depends on backups you have kept.

Full Access, trials, and purchases

On iPhone and iPad, an eligible device gets a 14-day Full Access trial starting when the first Deck is successfully saved. Starting the trial does not charge you. When it ends, your Vault remains readable, searchable, and exportable; creating, editing, and organizing require Full Access.

Full Access is a one-time purchase, not a subscription. If you already bought it, use the Apple account associated with the purchase and open Settings > Plans & Access > Restore Purchase. Restoring a purchase restores access, not your Vault data.

If restoration fails, check your connection and send support the error message. For an App Store refund request, follow Apple's refund instructions. Apple handles App Store payments and refund decisions. BiDecks does not currently offer a paid Full Access purchase on Android.

Feedback and feature requests

Have an idea for BiDecks, general feedback, or a product enquiry? Email admin@team5.dev. Tell us what you want to do and how the change would help. You can learn more about the product studio at team5.dev.
