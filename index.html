BiDecks

BiDecks keeps useful videos, articles, posts, and ideas in one personal place—ready when you want to try, make, learn, fix, or revisit something.

Save it. Remember why. Use it later.

Useful things are easy to find. Harder to find again.

A recipe trick in a Reel. A guitar lesson on YouTube. A DIY fix or an article worth returning to.

BiDecks gives the things you save a little of your own memory: why they mattered, what to try, what to remember, and where to pick up. Then, when the time comes, you can find the right one without searching through old feeds, saved posts, or browser history.

Quietly capable

From something you found to something you can use.

Share a useful find with BiDecks. Add what matters to you. Find it later and open it where you first found it.

01

Save from the apps you already use

Share a YouTube video, Instagram or Facebook post, TikTok, Reddit discussion, podcast, or article to BiDecks. Add your own context; open the original when you want to watch, listen, or read.

02

Keep your reason with the source

Add notes, purpose, takeaways, checklists while the idea is fresh. Attach files, images, or accessory links as needed.

03

Keep the exact moment

Mark a useful moment in a supported video or podcast. When you come back, open the original source at that moment where supported.

04

Add just enough structure

Use Topics for enduring interests and Try Lists for things you want to act on.

05

Find it in your own words

Search titles, source details, notes, takeaways across one library, then open the original video, post, or article to put it to use
